## The Problem

Wildfires move faster than human spotters; the first 30 minutes decide whether an ignition stays under an acre or levels a neighborhood. Satellite revisit latency is hours and resolution is 375 m. Mountaintop camera networks are fixed-viewpoint and blind to canyon and WUI ignitions. The catastrophic false-negative cost — a Marshall Fire repeat, a season of $10B+ insured losses, lives — dwarfs every other cost in the loop combined.

## The Wedge

In the Gunnison Valley + Crested Butte corridor — high-elevation montane, beetle-killed timber, sharp wildland-urban interface — we patrol with hardware already in hand: DJI Mavic Mini, Mac mini, two Raspberry Pis on Tailscale. Phase-0 cost is zero. Phase 0.5 ($215) adds RTL-SDR, PMS5003 smoke sensor, BME688 fire-weather, two Heltec Meshtastic radios, and a Pi 5 AI HAT+. Phase 1 ($2,613) is an autonomous Holybro X500 V2 + Cube Orange+ + Jetson Orin Nano Super + FLIR Lepton 3.5 stack with uAvionix Remote ID.

## The Moat

Five things in the repo today that are hard to replicate:

- **Multimodal edge fusion gate** — RGB + LWIR + acoustic + behavioral wildlife reasoned about jointly on a $249 Jetson, behind a single `wildfire_signal v1` schema.
- **N-drone swarm with k-of-N consensus + lossy-comms model** — three drones over a 1 km² mission produced a CONFIRMED smoke event at risk_score 97.33, `recommended_action=notify_fire_dept`, when 2 of 3 independently saw the plume within a 75 m / 60 s window.
- **GNSS-denied vision navigation** — VO + TRN + IMU + complementary fusion + GPS-spoof discriminator. 60-second outage at 80 m AGL: fused position within 1.39 m mean / 2.15 m max of truth.
- **TAK / Cursor-on-Target XML emitter** — every signal is one stdlib socket call from ATAK, FreeTAKServer, Anduril Lattice, Palantir Apollo, or any TAK-federated platform. One wire format, three universes.
- **NDAA / Blue-UAS-substitutable BOM** — every component has a documented Blue-UAS lineage path; Section 848 diligence is pre-done.
