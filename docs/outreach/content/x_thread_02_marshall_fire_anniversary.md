**Tweet 1/6** (anniversary hook)
Five years ago today the Marshall Fire burned more than 1,000 structures in Boulder County in a single afternoon. December. 100 mph winds. Grass-driven. The first 30 minutes is when WUI fire stops being containable and starts being a disaster.

**Tweet 2/6** (the gap)
ALERTCalifornia (1,240 fixed cameras), GOES/VIIRS (~375m resolution, hours of revisit), 911 calls (slow, often after the smoke is already large). All real, all helpful, all blind to canyon and WUI ignitions for the first 30 minutes.

**Tweet 3/6** (what we did about it)
I have been building wildfire-watch — open-source autonomous-drone civilian patrol layer, AOR is the Gunnison Valley + Crested Butte corridor in Colorado. Apache-2.0. Multimodal edge fusion (RGB + LWIR + acoustic + behavioral wildlife) on a $249 Jetson.

**Tweet 4/6** (the moat, briefly)
Swarm + k-of-N consensus voting (false-positive suppression). GNSS-denied vision-nav (smoke kills GPS lock). TAK / Cursor-on-Target interop (works with ATAK, Lattice, Apollo). Blue UAS-substitutable BOM (the diligence is pre-done). 240 tests, ~13,700 LOC.

**Tweet 5/6** (Colorado-specific)
Beetle-killed lodgepole and Engelmann spruce across the GMUG National Forest is multi-decade fuel-load amplification. The Marshall Fire happened in grass; the next one could happen in dead timber. Same WUI dynamics, higher fuel load.
