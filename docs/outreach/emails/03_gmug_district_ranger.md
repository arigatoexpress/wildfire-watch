Ranger TBD,

I am writing to coordinate with the Gunnison Ranger District before I fly anything over National Forest land in the Crested Butte corridor. The project is research and the flights are not commercial, but I want to give the District the chance to weigh in early rather than hear about it later. Coordination, not authorization, is what I am after — I understand the District is a stakeholder, not a permitting authority for non-commercial UAS over GMUG.

The project is wildfire-watch (https://github.com/arigatoexpress/wildfire-watch). Open-source civilian wildfire-detection research, AOR is the Gunnison Valley plus Crested Butte corridor, hardware in Phase 0 is a DJI Mavic Mini plus a Mac and two Raspberry Pis. Phase 1 (later this year, $2,600 BOM) is a Holybro X500 V2 with a Cube Orange+, a Jetson Orin Nano Super, an FLIR Lepton 3.5 thermal payload, and uAvionix Remote ID. Open multimodal-frame dataset (RGB + LWIR + acoustic + GPS-tagged behavioral) is a planned 2027 deliverable.

The wilderness piece is non-negotiable on my side. West Elk Wilderness, Maroon Bells-Snowmass Wilderness, and Raggeds Wilderness are encoded in my mission planner as hard exclusion polygons under 36 CFR 261.16. Any geofence breach is a hard stop in the simulator and will be a hard stop in flight. The first proposed test polygon is the Slate River drainage west of Mt. Crested Butte, ~1 km², well outside any wilderness boundary, on USFS land that is already a heavily-used recreation corridor.

Three specific things I would like the District's input on if your schedule allows.

First, beetle-kill ground truth. CSFS publishes beetle-kill polygons at coarse resolution. The District almost certainly has a finer working knowledge of where standing-dead stands are most concentrated above the Slate River and in the Cement Creek drainage. Even rough guidance helps prioritize where to fly first.

Second, sage grouse leks and big-game seasonal closures. Colorado Revised Statute 33-14.5 plus USFS guidance both apply, and I want to make sure the flight calendar respects rut/lekking windows.

Third, would the District want a copy of the post-flight imagery and the sensor-fusion event log for any flight over GMUG land? My default plan is to publish them open. If the District has a different preference (embargo period, redacted coordinates, share-back-but-not-publish), tell me and I will build that into the workflow.

No flights over GMUG land until I have my Part 107, LAANC pre-auth at KGUC, and an LOA from CBFPD. That puts realistic first-flight in early-to-mid summer.

No rush on this email. Happy to come by the District office in Gunnison whenever it is convenient.
