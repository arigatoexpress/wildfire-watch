Operator runbook

What this is

Operator console for the wildfire-watch volunteer drone fleet covering the Gunnison Valley + Crested Butte corridor in Gunnison County, Colorado. Signals shown here are emitted by drones (Mavic Mini today, Holybro X500 in Phase 1) and ground sensors (Pi heartbeats), then validated against the v1 wildfire signal schema and written to a JSONL sink.

Reading the dashboard

  • KPI strip — at a glance: 24h / 7d / all-time signal counts, the zone with the highest average risk score, last sensor heartbeat, and the on-disk retry-queue depth (if non-zero, the webhook is failing).
  • Live signal map — Leaflet over OpenStreetMap. The blue polygons are AOR zones from the canonical GeoJSON. Markers are colored by risk: green < 50, amber 50-74, red >= 75. Heartbeats render as small grey markers.
  • Sensor health — per-drone last-seen and signal counts. Default thresholds: online = < 30 min, stale = 30-120 min, offline = older; production can tune these via environment variables.
  • Signals table — most recent 100 by default. Filter by zone, signal type, or minimum risk score.

Deploying a sensor

  1. Phase 0 (free): use a Mavic Mini for scout flights, post-process on the Mac with python3 -m ml.fire_detection.mavic_post_flight. This emits validated signals to the JSONL sink that this dashboard reads.
  2. For a Pi heartbeat sensor: clone the repo on rari1 / rari2, run python3 -m ground_station.pi_telemetry_collector. It POSTs HMAC-signed signals to the configured webhook; failures are buffered to data/webhook_retry/.
  3. Phase 0.5 / Phase 1 hardware (RTL-SDR, PMS5003, Holybro X500) are documented in docs/intel/low-cost-hardware-2026-05-01.md and hardware/bom.csv.

Interpreting risk + actions

Risk score is a 0-100 composite of confidence, signal type, fire-weather index, and proximity to the wildland-urban interface. Recommended actions escalate from log_only through notify_operator, loiter_and_capture, notify_fire_dept, rtl. Fan-out to the fire department is operator-supervised — never autonomous.

What the AOR covers

Five zones: Slate River drainage, Cement Creek drainage, East River corridor, Long Lake / Lake Irwin, and the West Elk Wilderness exclusion (hard no-fly per 36 CFR 261.16). See AOR.md for fuel load, climate, regulatory, and partner-FD detail.
